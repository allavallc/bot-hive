## Goal

In-review column becomes a high-signal scan surface:

- **Group by Feature Set.** Section header per FS (e.g. "FS-022 — Swarm health core"); cards under that header.
- **Sort by Priority within each group.** High → Medium → Low. Tied priorities by created date (oldest first).
- **Grey out tickets with unfinished `Blocked by:`.** Reduced opacity (~0.4), `cursor: not-allowed`, click disabled. Tooltip on hover names the blocking ticket.
- **Mascot switches per `User-facing`.** `yes` → human mascot (existing). `no` → bot mascot (new). Lets you eyeball "tester bot's job" vs "my job" instantly.

- **`src/app/projects/[id]/board.client.tsx`** — group + sort + grey-out logic in the in-review column rendering; mascot switch based on `fm["User-facing"]`.
