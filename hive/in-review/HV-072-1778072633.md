## Goal
Fix two visual problems on the post-Accept/Reject pending-transition card state, observed in production after HV-055 + HV-059 shipped together:

1. The `↻ Rejected pending merge` badge was wrapping mid-word — text broke into `↻ pending` / `REJECTED merge` because the inline-flex pill couldn't fit alongside the priority/effort badges in the cramped cardTop row.
2. The walking-human mascot (HV-059) animated around the card border at the same time the pending badge was visible, overlapping it visually because both occupy the top-right corner.

## Why
A user clicked Reject on HV-070, saw an unreadable broken-up "↻ pending REJECTED merge" badge plus a walking-human animating across it. The visual signal that should say "your click registered, deploy is rolling" instead read as "something is broken." Fix is small but high-value — this is the visual feedback the human relies on during the 3-5 min wait between click and final state.

## Done when
- Pending banner moves to its own row in the card layout, between `cardTop` (id + priority/effort badges) and `cardFs` (feature-set label).
- The banner is full-width within the card, `white-space: nowrap`, with `overflow: hidden; text-overflow: ellipsis` for very narrow viewports (defensive).
- Banner reads `✓ Approved — pending merge` (green) or `✗ Rejected — pending merge` (orange).
- Walking-human (in-review) and walking-robot (in-progress) mascots are suppressed when `pendingTransition` is set — the card is transitioning, so the "this needs human attention" / "a bot is working here" signals are no longer accurate. Mascots return when pending clears.
- typecheck + lint + tests + build all clean.

## Out of scope
- Animation polish on the banner (slide-in, pulse, etc.). The 220ms fade-in is enough for now.
- Restyling the priority/effort badges. They're fine where they are.
- Showing the rejection reason or PR URL on the card. The detail panel handles that.

## Verification

- Manual: Accept a dummy ticket; same behavior with green banner.
- Manual: Confirm the banner clears when the underlying state actually transitions.
