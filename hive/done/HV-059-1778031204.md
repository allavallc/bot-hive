## Goal
Add a "walking-human" mascot animation to ticket cards in the **In-Review** column, analogous to the walking-robot mascot already on in-progress cards. Visually signals "this card needs human attention" at a glance, distinct from "a bot is working on this."

## Why
Today the In-Review column blends in with the rest of the board — same card style, no visual cue that these are the cards waiting on the human's accept/reject. With multiple tickets in flight, a human glancing at the board has no way to tell at a glance "these three need ME" vs "these three are bots working."

The walking-robot mascot (HV-036) already does this for in-progress cards: a small animating robot makes "a bot is working here" instantly readable. The mirror for in-review — a walking *human* — would do the same for "human action needed here."

It's a small visual addition with a real attention-direction payoff.

## Done when

- New `WalkingHuman` component (analogous to existing `WalkingRobot` in `src/components/robot-mascot.tsx`).
  - Reuses the same animation pattern (CSS `botRobotWalk` keyframes or a parallel keyframe set).
  - Visual: a tiny human silhouette (head + body + legs) walking, similar styling to the bot robot but obviously different shape.
  - Color: distinct from bot colors — probably a single "human" color (e.g., a warm tone) since there's only one human concept in this view.
- Card component (`board.client.tsx`) renders `<WalkingHuman />` when `ticket.state === "in-review"`, similar to how `<WalkingRobot />` renders on `in-progress`.
- Animation timing: similar to the bot mascot, with a randomized start delay so multiple cards don't pulse in sync.
- typecheck + lint + tests + build all clean.

## Desired output
A user opens the board and immediately sees:
- Bot mascots walking on **In-Progress** cards (existing — bots are working)
- Human mascot walking on **In-Review** cards (new — your turn)

The visual asymmetry maps to the action asymmetry. No more "wait, which ones do I need to do something about?"

## Success signals
- Humans report "I can see at a glance which cards need me."
- The two mascots are visually distinct enough that they're never confused.
- No layout regressions (mascots don't overlap card content).

## Failure signals
- The walking-human looks similar enough to the walking-robot that users can't tell them apart.
- Animation is too distracting / loud (looks like notifications popping).
- On in-review cards with a real handle (`Assigned to: allavallc-cc1 (...)`), the bot badge AND the walking human BOTH appear — visually noisy. Mitigation: it's fine; the bot badge says "this was allavallc-cc1's work" while the walking human says "now it's your turn to review." Two pieces of info, both relevant.

## Tests

- Manual: find an in-progress ticket. Confirm the walking-robot is still there (no regression).
- Manual: find a done/backlog ticket. Confirm no mascots (no regression).
