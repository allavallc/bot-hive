## Goal
The bot handle badge (currently rendered in the meta row alongside the FS badge) sits at the top-left corner of the ticket card, attached to the card's top border — like a Trello-style colored label tab. Visually attributes work to a bot at a glance without consuming a layout row.

## Why
Today the badge is in the meta row (`.cardMeta`) below the priority/effort row, sharing space with the FS badge. Visually it's just another inline badge — easy to miss. Moving it to the top-left edge makes the bot identity immediately scannable: looking at a column of cards, you can sweep down the left edge and see who owns what.

## Done when
- The bot handle badge is positioned absolutely at the top-left of the `.card` element, overlapping the top border (the badge "cuts" the border line).
- The badge keeps its deterministic color from `robotColor(handle)` — same as today.
- The `.cardMeta` row is removed (or simplified to only wrap the FS badge, if that's cleaner).
- The card layout doesn't shift for tickets without a handle (i.e., cards with no badge look identical to today's).
- typecheck + lint + test + build all clean.

## Tests
- Manual: open the live board. Cards with a real handle (e.g. mine: `nectar`, CC2's: `waggle`) show a small colored label at the top-left of each card, sitting on the border.
- Manual: cards without a handle (legacy `Assigned to: claude-opus-4-7`) show no label and look identical to before.
- Manual: hover over a card — no layout shift.
