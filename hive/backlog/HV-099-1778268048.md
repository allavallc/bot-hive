## Goal
A PM-role bot writes new tickets when given a high-level goal, breaks features into small pieces (≤ 5 files per ticket), sets `hive/focus.md` to direct other bots. Same Owner/Role primitives as tester — `Role: pm`. PM bots DAG-walk a queue of "things the human said to plan" rather than `backlog/`.

## Done when
- `hive/skills/pm.md` rubric — write tickets per HIVE.md format, break work into pieces small enough that a coder bot can DAG-walk and finish in one session
- PM-role detection in `my-work.sh` (mirror of HV-096 for tester)
- A "PM inbox" — somewhere the human says "we need feature X." Could be a file (`hive/pm-inbox.md`), a note channel, or a ticket. Decide as part of design.
- PM doesn't write code; it writes tickets. Its work-PR adds files in `hive/backlog/`.

## Notes
- PM is the most ambiguous role and the easiest to over-engineer. Defer until tester loop is stable.
- The PM must keep tickets small. Filed tickets that touch >5 files are a sign of bad breakdown.
- Future: PM can also retire stale tickets (move backlog/<old> to not-doing/ with reason).
