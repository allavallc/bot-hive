## Goal
After two rejections from the tester role on the same ticket, the next reject routes the ticket to `hive/blocked/` with `Failure mode: needs-human-review` instead of back to `in-progress/`. A note tagging the human goes to `hive/notes-to-humans/<tester>.log` so the human sees it on the panel.

## Done when
- Reject endpoint counts `Rejected by: <tester-handle>` events in the ticket's history (read from per-actor event logs OR from the ticket's `Rejected by:` field history if we track it)
- On the second reject: skip the move to `in-progress/`, instead route to `blocked/`. Update `**Failure mode**: needs-human-review`. Append a note tagging the human.
- Tester's first reject still sends the ticket back to `in-progress/` with reason; coder iterates once.
- Counter resets if the human resolves the block manually (moves the ticket out of `blocked/`).

## Notes
- The 2-rejection cap prevents the tester+coder loop from spinning forever. Two iterations is enough to surface "this needs a real human eye."
- The human's note ping uses the existing notes channel — no new infrastructure.
