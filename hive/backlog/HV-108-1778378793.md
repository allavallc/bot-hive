## Goal

Add a small animated bug to the border of any card whose ticket is tagged as a bug, with a tiny robot a short distance behind it, both walking around the perimeter of the card. The animation makes bug-state tickets instantly recognizable on the board and adds personality to the kanban.

## Detection — what counts as a "bug" card

For v1, treat a ticket as a bug if its title starts with `Bug:` (case-insensitive) or its body contains `**Failure mode**:` followed by non-empty content. (Most existing ticket bodies have the field even when empty; the non-empty check is what flags a real bug ticket.)

Future: a dedicated `**Type**: bug` frontmatter field could replace the heuristic. Out of scope for v1.

## Concrete UX

- Card border gets two small SVG/emoji elements walking the perimeter:
  - A bug (e.g. 🐞 or a small custom SVG) leading
  - A robot (the existing `robot-mascot` component reused, scaled down) chasing ~30-50px behind
- Both walk in the same direction (e.g. clockwise), continuous loop
- Speed slow enough not to distract — ~12-14s per full perimeter loop, matching the existing `botRobotWalk` / `cardHumanWalk` animations in `board.module.css`
- Pointer-events none so they don't block clicks on the card
