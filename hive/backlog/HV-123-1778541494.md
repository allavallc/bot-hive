## Concrete repro (live, 2026-05-11)

- Colony: 2 bots — `allavallc.buzz` (1/2, older, currently PM+tester per role math) and `allavallc.wren` (2/2, currently coder).
- Buzz built and shipped HV-112 (`User-facing: no`). Per the new routing, tester reviews.
- Buzz IS the tester. Buzz built it. Rubric forbids self-review (`hive/skills/tester.md` lines 75 + 89).
- Wren can't review either — wren is coder, not tester.
- HV-112 sits in in-review with no actor able to review.

## Root cause

The 2-bot role consolidation table (`hive/roles.md`, ADR-002) assigns PM + tester to the older bot, assuming the dev work flows through the newer bot. When the older bot is itself a dev bot (because of HV-122's mis-assignment OR because the colony was single-bot historically and the older bot built before bot 2 spawned), the constraint "tester ≠ dev" can't be satisfied.

## Fix paths (pick one)

- **Option A — escalate to the human as a tester fallback.** When the 2-bot colony hits a deadlock (in-review ticket whose dev bot is also the only available tester), surface a panel notice: "HV-XXX needs a human reviewer — no eligible bot tester in this colony." The human accepts via the existing Accept button. Treat human-review as the always-available fallback.
- **Option B — auto-promote the dev bot's role for THIS ticket.** Allow the dev bot to mark "I built this" and route to the OTHER bot regardless of role. Complex; couples role to ticket.
- **Option C — require 3-bot colony for User-facing: no review.** Document the limitation. Until a third bot joins, all `User-facing: no` tickets escalate to human. Cleanest semantically but reduces 2-bot value.

Recommend **A** + **C combined**: in a 2-bot colony, `User-facing: no` ALWAYS escalates to human Accept (treating bot-tester as a 3+ feature). Document this as the 2-bot semantic. The deadlock disappears.

## Done when
