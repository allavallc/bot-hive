## Goal

Two distinct review paths through `hive/in-review/`, routed by the `User-facing` frontmatter field:

- `User-facing: yes` → human reviews + approves via the Accept button on the board → done/.
- `User-facing: no` → tester bot reviews against "Done when" → moves to done/ (or rejects to in-progress/ with a reason).

One folder, two actors, no UI change required by this ticket (HV-113 handles the visual side).

## Concrete changes

- **`scripts/in-review.{sh,ps1}`** — refuse to move a ticket to in-review/ if `User-facing` field is empty. Force the dev bot to set it explicitly.
- **`hive/skills/tester.md`** — update the rubric: "Tester bot reviews ONLY `User-facing: no` tickets in in-review. `User-facing: yes` tickets are the human's job; do not touch them."
- **`hive/HIVE.md`** — document the routing rule in the in-review section.
